## Etsy setup notes

- **Digital download**, not physical. Etsy delivers the file automatically — no
  manual sending, no shipping profile.
- **Shop name** must be free: `OMNEXStudio`, `OMNEXVisuals`, `StudioOMNEX`.
- Etsy holds payouts from a new shop for a short period. Expected, not a problem.
- **Do not price below €15.** On Etsy a very low price on a digital product reads as
  low quality and suppresses conversion rather than raising it.
- Add all three listings in one sitting; the shop looks abandoned with one.
