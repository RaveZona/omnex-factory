LUXURY COSMETICS — PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY BACKGROUNDS
=================================================

Inside this pack:
- /images  — premium empty background scenes (PNG, 1024px+), square and portrait
- Each scene has a clean, centred space to place your product
- manifest.json — the exact scene list

HOW TO USE
1. Open a background in any editor (Canva, Photoshop, Photopea — free)
2. Cut out your product photo and drop it into the centred space
3. Add a soft shadow under it to match the scene's light
4. Export and publish

Best results: shoot your product on a plain background first, remove the
background (remove.bg is free), then composite onto these scenes.

These are AI-generated scenes. See LICENSE.txt for usage terms.
