# Revenue matrix

Every number here is derived from inputs that are visible and editable in
`engine/scripts/scan_intel.py`. If the price looks optimistic, change one field and
the payback moves. That is the only form of financial projection worth committing to
a repository.

**Ranked by payback, not by revenue.** A self-funding sequence pays for the ambitious
item with the margin from the boring one. Building highest-priority-first is how a
team spends its runway on the thing it was most excited about.

## What these numbers are not

They are estimates of a market nobody has surveyed, at prices nobody has been charged.
The build costs are grounded — they come from a stated day rate and a stated size — and
the revenue figures are judgement. The ordering is more reliable than the magnitudes,
because the ordering depends mostly on build cost and that is the half we can compute.
