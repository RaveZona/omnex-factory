## Connector note — what can actually be automated

**Fiverr: no.** There is no public seller API. Gigs, orders, messages and
delivery all happen through their interface, and their terms require a real
person operating the account. Route A therefore always needs you at the point
of accepting and delivering an order. Anything claiming to automate a Fiverr
seller account is either scraping or a ToS violation, and both end in a ban.

**Upwork: barely.** An API exists, but automated proposal submission is against
their terms in practice, and proposals cost connects. Same conclusion as Fiverr.

**Etsy: yes.** Etsy has a real Open API v3 — listings can be created, updated and
priced, and digital downloads are delivered automatically by the platform.

**Lemon Squeezy: yes**, and you already have an account. Full API: create
products and variants, set prices, attach files, receive orders by webhook.

**Gumroad: yes**, similar, higher fee.

So the split is not about which site is better. It is about what is being sold:
a **service** needs a human at the order; a **product** does not. Route B is the
one that can run without you once it is listed.
