## Licence is a gate, not a modifier

`facebookresearch/HyperAgents` is a genuine research contribution from a corporate lab
and is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — non-commercial, with a ShareAlike term that would
infect anything derived from it. It cannot be built on by a business at any price, so
it scores zero on the licence axis regardless of quality. `saltbo/agent-kanban` is
FSL-1.1-ALv2 and becomes Apache-2.0 only after two years. An unknown licence is treated
as no: a repository with no LICENSE file is all rights reserved, not public domain.
