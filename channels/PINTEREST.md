## How to post so it actually works

- **Pin 3–5 per day, not 10 at once.** A burst from a new account reads as spam.
- **Every pin needs its own description.** Duplicated text gets suppressed.
- **Vertical only.** 2:3 is the format Pinterest ranks; our 896x1344 is already close.
- **Link every pin.** A pin without a destination cannot convert.
- Results are slow and then durable: **first clicks in days, real traffic in weeks**,
  and pins keep working for months. That is the opposite of a feed post.
